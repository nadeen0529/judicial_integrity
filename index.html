WHAT IT MEANS

What is Judicial Integrity?

Judicial integrity means that judges and the judicial system act fairly, honestly, and independently. It requires that verdicts be guided solely by law and verified facts—never by bribes, political pressure, or personal relationships.

When courts uphold integrity, citizens know that disputes will be settled fairly, contracts will be enforced, and constitutional rights will be defended equally for every person.
